Repository purpose

Policy research as a revisable public record

Each publication separates what the evidence supports, what it does not support, and what would change the result.

Exact findings

Verdicts retain their scope, confidence and evidence date instead of becoming promotional claims.

Visible limits

Counterevidence, uncertainty and missing measures remain part of the reader experience.

Controlled change

Feedback can trigger review, but does not silently alter an accepted finding.
