Australia, AI, jobs and entry/re-entry work to 2035

Will AI and robotics mainly displace or reshape entry and re-entry work—or can they open viable business-creation pathways across age groups?

  • Evidence available to 21 July 2026
  • Three reports
  • Australia

Cumulative series result

NOT SUPPORTED ON CURRENT EVIDENCE

The creation pathway is real enough to test, but current evidence does not show that it is larger than displacement and augmentation, that targeted support is sufficient to make it dominant and higher quality, or that the pathway and its support would survive an enduring shock.

What this does not mean: The result does not say that AI-enabled creation is absent, that every job effect is harmful, that targeted support has no value, or that the hypothesis is false.
